<h1 align="center">About Me</h1>

![Professional Photo](image-ProfessionalPhoto.jpg)

Hello, my name is Sanjay Kashyap. I am currently an undergraduate student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, majoring in mechanical engineering with a specialized concentration in aerospace engineering and a minor in mathematics. My ultimate professional aspiration is to design next-generation gas turbine engines—an ambition deeply rooted in my upbringing. Growing up in a dedicated military family with both of my parents serving in the Air Force, I was constantly surrounded by aviation and advanced defense technologies. My father served as a jet engine mechanic, and some of my fondest childhood memories involve visiting the maintenance shops where he worked, standing in awe before giant turbojet and turbofan engines. Being surrounded by various military aircraft for my entire life up until leaving for college naturally inspired my lifelong fascination with flight and led me directly to my chosen aerospace focus.

To me, being an engineer means actively contributing to the future of society. Almost every object, product, or system we interact with in our daily lives exists because an engineer took the time to analyze, design, and optimize it. For instance, consider something as ordinary as a bed. An industrial engineer designed the specialized textile manufacturing equipment that processes and weaves threads into soft bed sheets, while a mechanical engineer designed the durable structural frame that securely supports the mattress. Engineering seamlessly connects subtle everyday conveniences to immense technological breakthroughs.

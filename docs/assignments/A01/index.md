## Analyze
#### Task A: Portfolio analysis
###### Portfolio 1: https://3-d-portfolio-lilac-xi.vercel.app/#work

This online portfolio is excellently organized, making it easy for users to navigate and view the portfolio owner’s skills, projects, and previous experience as they scroll through the page. The short animations used to introduce the owner’s information add a polished and engaging element to the design, demonstrating a level of effort and attention to detail that is often lacking in online portfolios. Additionally, the navigation bar in the top-right corner allows users to quickly access whichever section they are interested in. The overall color scheme is also visually appealing and easy on the eyes, creating a pleasant experience for the user.

###### Portfolio 2: https://javiersanchezramirez.github.io/

This online portfolio is similar to the first portfolio analyzed in that it allows you to scroll through the entire page but also has a bar at the top to skip to different sections. One thing I particularly like about this resume is that one of the first things shown is the pictures of the portfolio owner's projects and when you click on the on a project's picture, you are taken to the details and description of that project. The project pictures can also be filtered to by the different categories the projects fall under.

# A3 – Parametric and FEA

## Objective
The purpose of this assignment is to parametrically design a bar that is subjected to an axial load between 300 lbf and 500 lbf such that the bar's maximum axial deflection does not exceed 0.009 inches. The bar is to be designed from aluminum with a Young's modulus in the range of 8.5–11.5 × 10⁶ psi. Then we are supposed to run an FEA simulation on the CAD model we created and afterwards reflect on the differences between the calculations used to design the model and what the FEA simulation shows.
